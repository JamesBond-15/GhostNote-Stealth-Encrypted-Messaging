# GhostNote Roadmap

**GhostNote** is being developed with a long‑term focus on **privacy integrity, architectural stability, and responsible expansion**.  

Growth is intentional.  
Features are introduced only when they strengthen privacy — not when they maximize engagement.

---

## Phase 1 — Stealth Foundation (Current)
- Privacy‑first communication philosophy  
- User‑owned identity concept  
- Zero‑contact onboarding direction  
- Standalone Android application  
- Core messaging reliability improvements  

---

## Phase 2 — Stability & Hardening
- Message lifecycle refinement  
- Infrastructure trust minimization  
- Identity reliability improvements  
- Network resilience upgrades  
- Privacy‑preserving onboarding enhancements  

---

## Phase 3 — Secure Group Communication
- Secure multi‑participant messaging  
- Group lifecycle management  
- Controlled message expiration models  
- Privacy‑preserving group identity boundaries  

---

## Phase 4 — Privacy‑Preserving Media
- Secure media delivery improvements  
- Controlled retention and expiration  
- Storage minimization strategies  
- Media lifecycle automation  

---

## Phase 5 — Professional Communication Spaces
- Secure workspace communication concepts  
- Identity compartmentalization models  
- Long‑term communication integrity improvements  

---

## Phase 6 — Ecosystem Readiness
- Cross‑device identity continuity  
- Long‑term cryptographic agility  
- Privacy‑preserving extensibility design  
- Infrastructure independence planning  

---

**GhostNote is not being rushed.  
It is being prepared.**
